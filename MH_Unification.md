*This framework-neutral standard judges a theory by its mathematical compression, logical rigor, and prospective empirical exposure—regardless of what it says the universe is made of.*

  - *The Falsifier:* The discovery of any adjustable, free parameter embedded in the foundational math, or a derived numerical value that contradicts established, high-precision measurements.

- **Criterion 3: Prospectively Sealed Empirical Exposure**
  - *What must be proven:* Explicit, quantitative predictions of novel physical phenomena or measurements made *before* the experimental data is collected or observed.
  - *The Falsifier:* A single high-precision, preregistered experiment or cosmological observation that directly contradicts the predicted quantitative value.
- **Criterion 4: Absolute Immobility (No Auxiliary Patches)**
  - *What must be proven:* The framework's equations must be rigid; modifying a single axiom must mathematically shatter the entire system.
  - *The Falsifier:* The introduction of *ad hoc* variables, new dimensions, or invisible fields after the fact to retroactively absorb a conflicting experimental result.
- **Criterion 5: Reproducible Path and Causal Transparency**
  - *What must be proven:* A complete, open-source mathematical derivation where any independent auditor starting from the axioms inevitably arrives at the exact same physical consequences.
  - *The Falsifier:* The identification of a logical leap, an un-derived mathematical assumption, or a region of ambiguity where the output does not strictly follow from the input.
